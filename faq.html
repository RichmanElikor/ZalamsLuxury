Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can use this to track your package on our courier's website. Tracking usually becomes active within 24 hours of shipment.

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. After that, the order enters processing and cannot be changed. Please contact us immediately at hello@zalams.com if you need to make changes.

Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. You will receive an email confirmation once your order has been processed and shipped.

Standard delivery costs ₦5,000. Orders over ₦50,000 qualify for free delivery. Express delivery (1–2 business days) costs ₦10,000 and is available in Lagos and Abuja.

Yes! We deliver to all states across Nigeria. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days within Lagos and 5–7 business days for other states.

International shipping is currently not available but we are working on it. Sign up for our newsletter to be notified when we launch international delivery.

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed and in their original packaging with all tags attached. Sale items are non-refundable.

Email us at hello@zalams.com with your order number and reason for return. We'll send you a return label within 24 hours. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days of receiving the returned item.

Each product page has a size guide with detailed measurements. We recommend measuring yourself and comparing with our size chart. If you're between sizes, we suggest sizing up for a more comfortable fit.

Care instructions are on the label of each garment. Generally, we recommend cold machine wash or hand wash, avoiding bleach, and air drying to maintain the quality and longevity of your pieces.

Some of our pieces are limited edition and may not be restocked. Popular styles are restocked periodically. Subscribe to our newsletter to get notified when your favourite pieces are back in stock.
